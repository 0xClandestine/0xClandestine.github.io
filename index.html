About

I enjoy making computers do less work.

I'm self-taught: business degree, no CS background. I learned to program between lectures from Stack Overflow and docs — in the pre-AI era, so you had to actually figure things out. The entry point was a personal project, automating my trading portfolio to cut risk.

Solidity clicked when I wanted the same on-chain, with Uniswap, Sushi and the early dapps. That's the real appeal of Ethereum: anyone can build a censorship-resistant app that, done properly, lasts.

When gas got expensive, bad design meant an unusable app, so I went deep on optimization — which pulled me into the fundamentals of computer science. You can't optimize primitives you don't understand.

Some contracts I've written have held billions in deposits at their peak, without loss of funds. The work hasn't changed: design the protocol, then write code that stays correct while real money moves through it.
